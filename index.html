ZENSTRE — service & complaint management platform

Nov 2024 – Present

Built for solar, inverter, and battery companies to coordinate customer support and field mechanics in one place.

React.jsTypeScript Node.jsExpress.jsJWT MongoDBSocket.IO AWS EC2 · NginxClean Architecture
  • Three roles — Admin, Coordinator, and Mechanic — each with their own access and workflow, and no open sign-up: accounts only exist once an admin creates them.
  • Complaints are auto-assigned to mechanics by a routing algorithm that weighs current workload and specialization, rather than a flat queue.
  • Mechanics can decline a job, but have to give a reason — that record is what makes accountability possible later.
  • Live chat between Admin–Coordinator and Coordinator–Mechanic over Socket.IO, plus group video calls (ZEGOCLOUD) for remote troubleshooting.
  • An AI chatbot on the landing page handles first-line product questions before a human needs to step in.
  • Deployed on AWS EC2 behind an Nginx reverse proxy; code is organized around Clean Architecture so the business logic doesn't depend on the framework around it.
